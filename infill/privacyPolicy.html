Privacy policy

Infill is a free parametric 3D CAD app for Android. It works offline, requires no account, and stores nothing in the cloud. Your models stay on your device. Infill sends only anonymous usage, crash, and performance reports to Google Firebase.

Effective date: 16 November 2026

About Infill

Infill is developed by Jakub Piasecki. For privacy questions, contact contact@jpiasecki.com.

Your models and files

Infill stores your projects, models, and settings on your device. The developer does not receive this information and does not operate a server that contains users' projects.

When you import or export a file, Infill accesses only the file you select through Android's system file picker.

Infill is free, does not require an account or an internet connection, and does not contain advertising.

Analytics and diagnostics

Infill uses Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Performance Monitoring to understand how the app is used and to find crashes and slowdowns. These services collect which screens are opened and which features are used, crash logs and stack traces, app start and rendering timings, device model and operating system version, app version, and device and app identifiers, including an app instance identifier and the Android advertising ID.

Infill does not send your models, project names, file names, or file contents to these services.

When your device is offline, reports wait on the device and are sent when a connection is available. Reports are encrypted in transit and are processed by Google on the developer's behalf under Google's privacy policy. They are not used for advertising. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Data sharing and sale

Infill does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with advertisers. Google processes the Firebase information described above as a service provider to the developer.

Retention and deletion

Your projects and settings remain on your device until you delete them, clear Infill's app data, or uninstall the app. Files you export stay where you saved them.

Google retains Firebase Analytics data linked to an app instance for up to 14 months, and Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring reports for 90 days.

Security

Infill keeps your data in Android's app storage, which other apps cannot access. Reports sent to Firebase are encrypted in transit.

Third-party services

Infill uses Google Firebase. Read Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's data practices.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when Infill's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
